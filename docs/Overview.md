# Overview

## Objective

Our objective is to predict the energy consumption of a household in Mexico using electricity consumption and weather data set 

## Expected added value 

This project can be usefull for househould to better understand energy consuption patterns, anticipate the periods with the highest consumption and try to reduce their energy cost.

## Data

The dataset encompasses information obtained from energy consumption in a residence located in the northeast region of Mexico over a span of 14 months. Each data point was recorded at one-minute intervals and features energy-related metrics enriched with weather data provided by OpenWeather, under a free license. 

The data we work on come from a smart meter (AT-Q-SY1 WiFi) device connected directly to the main energy of the household, then transmitted to a Rasberry Pi for storage and organization of the above with Python. This dataset was enriched with weather data provided by OpenWeather API.
